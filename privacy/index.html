Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026.

Short version: Charm City Sim collects no data about you.

What the game stores

Your progress (money, upgrades, the regulars you've met, settings) is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

No accounts or sign-in. No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data. No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. No messages to other players.

Purchases

The optional Full City unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. We use them only to fix bugs.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: Honu.game.co@gmail.com.
