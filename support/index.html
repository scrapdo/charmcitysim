Common questions

How do I get Full City?
Tap the teal Full City button next to Start shift on the start screen, or Access & controls → Unlock Full City. It's one purchase, once.

How do I restore Full City on a new phone?
Open the game, then Access & controls → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I was charged but nothing unlocked.
Try Restore purchases. If it still doesn't open, email us; purchases are handled by Apple and can also be refunded through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Only buying or restoring Full City talks to the App Store.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
